MISSION Առաքելություն

Structured online tutoring with verified Armenian educators, personalized learning plans, visible progress, and parent accountability.

Help children and adult learners receive high-quality, culturally connected, accountable education from qualified Armenian tutors — while giving parents a transparent view of what is being taught, what's been completed, where the student is struggling, and what happens next.

Evening in Yerevan, morning in the U.S. — one school day scheduled safely across the gap, by design.

THE PROBLEM Խնդիր

Right now, tutoring lives in five different apps.

Lessons in Zoom. Homework in WhatsApp. Payment tracked in someone's memory. A learning plan that exists only in the tutor's head — and disappears the day that tutor leaves.

Today, scattered

  • Vague, verbal feedback after class
  • Homework sent over WhatsApp
  • Payments tracked by hand
  • No record when a tutor changes
  • Progress that's hard to measure
  • Messaging that drifts outside the school entirely

With 2tor, one record

  • Written feedback after every lesson, on a schedule
  • Assignments, submissions, and resources in one place
  • Invoices, receipts, and a tutor ledger parents and tutors can both see
  • A learning plan that survives a tutor reassignment
  • Milestones with evidence, not just a grade
  • Conversations a parent can always open

“Software doesn't replace the tutor's judgment. It captures the evidence, enforces the workflow, and makes the progress visible.”

PRINCIPLES

Seven rules the product doesn't bend on.

01

Human-led education

The tutor decides what and how to teach. Software supports that judgment — it doesn't automate it away.

02

Parent visibility

Schedules, assignments, submissions, feedback, milestones, and billing — open to the parent who's paying for it.

03

Child safety by design

No private tutor-to-child messaging, ever. Every conversation a parent can see, an admin can review.

04

Measurable progress

A baseline, a goal, a plan, and milestone evidence — for every student, in every subject.

05

Operational control

The company verifies tutors, makes the match, sets the standard, and can act when something's wrong.

06

Fast and simple

Faster and easier than juggling Zoom, WhatsApp, and a spreadsheet. That's the whole bar.

07

Honest claims

No guaranteed grades, no guaranteed honesty. A real process, reported transparently.

ROADMAP Ծրագիր

Three horizons. Each one earns the next.

Automation and scale are deferred on purpose. Nothing in “Next” gets built until the operational backbone in “Now” is actually holding.

Now Private beta → launch

The operational backbone

  • Parent, student, tutor, and admin roles with real approval workflow
  • Timezone-safe scheduling with Zoom links, attendance, and reminders
  • Learning plans, assignments, milestones, and human feedback after every lesson
  • Parent-visible messaging — no private tutor-child chat
  • Stripe payments, invoices, and a tutor payout ledger
  • Verifiable parental consent before any child data is collected
GATE — 30 paying students through one month · ≥70% would recommend · no unresolved safety incident
Next After validation

Depth and retention

  • Tutor ratings, parent reviews, and short intro videos
  • Monthly packages, subscriptions, and group-course enrollment
  • Google/Apple login, passkeys, calendar sync
  • Skill taxonomy, question bank, student portfolios
  • Academic manager and dedicated support roles
Unlocked by proof, not by calendar date
Later Scale

Wider, not just bigger

  • Native mobile apps and native live video
  • Adaptive assessments and AI-assisted, human-reviewed learning plans
  • School and organization accounts, a public API
  • Multiple regions and managed, multi-instance infrastructure
Distraction until “Now” succeeds

NOT IN THE MVP, ON PURPOSE — microservices · native video conferencing · facial or emotion analysis · public leaderboards for children · unlimited unstructured student messaging · guaranteed-grade or guaranteed-no-cheating claims.

WHERE THE REAL WORK IS

The hard part was never the calendar.

Any team can build a schedule, a chat window, and a dashboard. The hard part is proving that parents trust the tutors, tutors consistently deliver quality, progress stays visible, pricing holds up, communication stays safe, and families stay enrolled. Build the operational backbone first. Add automation only after the human process works.
